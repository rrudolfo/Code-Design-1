Everything You Need to Know

Map Overview

Origins is a Zombies map set in a muddy World War I-style battlefield with trenches, bunkers, giant robots, and an underground excavation site. The map is much larger and more complex than many other Zombies maps because it includes generators, build able items, elemental staffs, hidden parts, and multiple Easter Egg steps. Players need to move carefully through mud, avoid giant robot feet, and manage zombies while completing objectives around the map.

Generators

Generators are one of the most important parts of Origins because they power key areas of the map. Instead of flipping a normal power switch, players must activate several generators located around the battlefield. While a generator is turning on, zombies attack the area, so players have to defend it until the process is complete. Turning on generators gives access to important machines, perks, Pack-a-Punch, and other parts of the Easter Egg.

The Staffs

The elemental staffs are the main special weapons in Origins. There are four staffs: Ice, Fire, Wind, and Lightning. Each staff has different parts hidden around the map, and each one has its own theme and attack style. After building the staffs, you can upgrade them by solving puzzles and completing steps around the map. Upgrading all four staffs is one of the most important requirements for completing the Easter Egg.

The Crazy Place

The Crazy Place is a strange hidden area connected to the elemental staffs. Players enter it through portals around the map using gramophones and records. This area is important because it is where many staff upgrade puzzles happen. The Crazy Place is dangerous because walls can shift, zombies still attack, and players can easily get trapped if they are not paying attention. It feels like the "mystery zone" of the map and connects heavily to the story of Samantha and the Easter Egg.

Easter Egg

The Origins Easter Egg is called Little Lost Girl, and it requires players to complete a long series of steps. The main goals include upgrading all four staffs, placing them in the correct locations, using special equipment like the G-Strike, filling soul boxes, and completing final objectives connected to Samantha. The Easter Egg requires teamwork, timing, and map knowledge because many steps happen in different locations. It is not something most players finish by accident; it takes planning and patience.

Survival Tips

Surviving Origins is difficult because the map has mud, tight pathways, powerful enemies, and the Panzer Soldat. Players should focus on opening the map early, turning on generators, getting Juggernog, and building a staff as soon as possible. Good communication helps a lot in co-op because players can split up to gather parts, defend generators, and call out robot movements. In solo, it is better to move slowly, save zombies at the end of rounds, and avoid starting too many objectives at once.
